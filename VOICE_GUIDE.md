# OtterAI Blog Voice Guide

## Our Voice: The Knowledgeable Friend

Write like you're a developer/entrepreneur friend sharing genuine insights over coffee. Not a company, not a salesperson, but someone who's been there and wants to help.

## Personality Traits

- **Curious** - "What if we tried this differently?"
- **Honest** - "This didn't work for me, but here's what did"
- **Enthusiastic** - "This completely changed how I think about..."
- **Practical** - "Here's exactly what I did"
- **Accessible** - "Let me break this down simply"

## Language Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ Say This:
- "Here's what I learned..."
- "This might surprise you..."
- "In my experience..."
- "Let me show you exactly how..."
- "The thing that changed everything was..."
- "Here's the honest truth..."
- "I used to think... but now..."
- "What happened next blew my mind"

### ❌ Not This:
- "We are pleased to announce..."
- "Our cutting-edge solution..."
- "Industry-leading platform..."
- "Seamlessly integrate..."
- "Leverage synergies..."
- "Best-in-class offering..."
- "Utilize our robust framework..."

## Sentence Starters That Work

### For Stories:
- "Last month, I..."
- "Picture this:"
- "I'll never forget the day..."
- "Three weeks ago, something happened that..."

### For Insights:
- "Here's what I wish someone had told me:"
- "The breakthrough came when I realized..."
- "What I discovered changed everything:"
- "The counterintuitive truth is..."

### For Practical Advice:
- "Here's exactly what you need to do:"
- "The fastest way I've found is..."
- "If I were starting over, I'd..."
- "The one thing that made the biggest difference was..."

## Tone Guidelines

### Be Personal, Not Corporate
- Use "I," "you," and "we" naturally
- Share your own experiences and opinions
- Admit when you're wrong or uncertain
- Show enthusiasm without being fake

### Be Specific, Not Vague
- "Saved 3 hours and 47 minutes" not "saved time"
- "23 out of 30 users" not "most users"
- "Last Tuesday at 2:47 PM" not "recently"
- "Increased conversions from 2.1% to 8.3%" not "improved performance"

### Be Conversational, Not Formal
- Use contractions: "you'll," "we're," "it's"
- Ask rhetorical questions
- Use everyday language
- Vary sentence length and structure

## Content Structure

### Hook Readers Immediately
Start with:
- A surprising statement
- A specific story
- A provocative question
- A relatable problem

### Keep Them Engaged
- Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Include subheadings that tell a story
- Add specific examples and screenshots
- Ask questions throughout

### End with Value
- Summarize key takeaways
- Provide next steps
- Share additional resources
- Invite engagement

## Examples in Action

### Instead of This:
"Implementing payment processing can be challenging for developers new to financial integrations. Our platform simplifies this process through streamlined APIs and comprehensive documentation."

### Write This:
"I still remember my first time adding payments to an app. I spent three days reading Stripe docs, broke my test environment twice, and almost gave up. Here's what I wish someone had shown me on day one."

### Instead of This:
"Users can leverage our AI-powered development environment to rapidly prototype applications with minimal technical overhead."

### Write This:
"Last week, I watched my designer friend build her entire portfolio site in 20 minutes. No coding. Just describing what she wanted to an AI. I'm still processing how wild that is."

## The Relatability Test

Before publishing, ask yourself:
- Would I send this to a friend who needed help with this topic?
- Does it sound like something I'd actually say?
- Am I solving a real problem real people have?
- Would someone share this because it's genuinely helpful?

## Quick Voice Check

Read your draft and count:
- Personal pronouns (I, you, we, my, your)
- Specific numbers and examples
- Questions (rhetorical or direct)
- Contractions and casual language

If these are missing, your content probably sounds too corporate.
